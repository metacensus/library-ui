# MetaCensus marks

The marks are the primary mark, the wordmark lockup, the arrow and the favicon, in [`marks/`](marks). All rights in them are reserved ([`marks/NOTICE.md`](marks/NOTICE.md)); the repository's AGPL-3.0-only license does not cover them. Questions and co-branded approvals: brand@metacensus.io.

## Permitted use

- Refer to MetaCensus, and link to its work, with the marks as provided here.
- Software built from this package that is MetaCensus's own, or that MetaCensus has approved, may carry the marks it ships.
- Anything else, including a fork or a derived product, removes the marks or asks first; a fork must not present itself as MetaCensus.

## Files

| File                  | Mark                                                                            |
| --------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `mark.svg`            | Primary mark: the horizontal bars. The default for app icons, UI and marketing. |
| `wordmark-lockup.svg` | Mark and wordmark, horizontal. The wordmark is outlined Inter, never live text. |
| `arrow.svg`           | The arrow, built from the mark's own bars.                                      |
| `favicon.svg`         | The mark for tab icons.                                                         |

## Rules

- **Clear space:** keep the height of the smallest bar clear on all sides.
- **Minimum size:** 24px tall on screen, 12mm in print, 16px for a favicon.
- **Variants:** solid `#3B0E75` and reversed white on dark, for the mark and the lockup; the lockup also has a gradient (`#37128A` to `#AB82EC`, left to right). The gradient needs a white or very light ground; use the reversed variant on dark or violet grounds.
- **Scale** proportionally from these vector files.
- **The arrow** may be rotated to follow reading direction. Nothing else may.

## Don'ts

- Don't rebuild or re-draw the marks, or re-set the wordmark in another typeface.
- Don't recolor outside the variants above, and don't use the coral signal color in a mark.
- Don't rotate (except the arrow), stretch, compress, skew or distort a mark, or alter the spacing between bars.
- Don't add gradients, shadows or effects beyond the brand gradient.
- Don't place the gradient lockup on a busy or low-contrast photograph.
